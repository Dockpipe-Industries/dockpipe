## Milestones and recommended MVP

1. **M0 — First route selected:** package-owned ScreenCaptureKit/VideoToolbox
   sender, Qt/FFmpeg viewer and endpoint-pinned TLS 1.3 over the existing broker's
   authenticated binary WebSocket relay. Keep media libraries out of generic core.
   Qualify dependency closure, permissions, bounds, provider terms and the exact
   Linux amd64 / Darwin arm64 pair. WebRTC dependency research remains a candidate
   for later direct connectivity.
2. **M1 — Native MVP in progress:** one monitor, baseline H.264, reliable viewing
   and mouse/keyboard control, independent view/input grants, revocation, cleanup,
   dedicated Qt view and CLI/API recovery. Prove the real existing Cloudflare path;
   do not equate local relay fixtures with deployed acceptance. TURN setup is
   deferred until another route needs it. Exclude clipboard, audio, advanced
   multi-monitor and adaptive codec switching from this slice.
3. **M2 — Workspace and platform parity:** independent Canvas/Tiling selection,
   scoped clipboard, remaining native capture/input backends, self-hosted TURN and
   RDP/VNC compatibility, with native permission/session-type acceptance evidence.
4. **M3 — Codec and deployment qualification:** comparable H.264/AV1/NLV-1 results,
   controlled network impairment and resource tests, BYOC/Cloud/on-prem operations,
   and a support matrix. Adaptive codec switching and audio remain later candidates.

Minimum measurement dimensions: encode/decode and input-to-display latency, network
traffic/bitrate, CPU/GPU/memory, frame rate/dropped frames and text readability for
static and changing desktops under bandwidth, jitter and loss variation. Keep
codec-only evidence separate from transport and full-session evidence.
